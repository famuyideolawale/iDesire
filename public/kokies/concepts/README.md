# Kokies gift concepts — separate from iDesire fabrics

Three new sample product entries, with original illustrative product photography generated using the built-in image-generation tool. No CLI fallback. These are completed gift items, not fabric listings. Price, exact specifications and availability require client confirmation.

## Files and primary inspiration

- `custom-lunch-bag.webp` — Customised Lunch Bag. Inspiration: https://www.instagram.com/kokiesworldofgifts/reel/DdrmqpPNWzl/
- `insulated-event-cooler.webp` — Insulated Event Cooler. Inspiration: https://www.instagram.com/kokiesworldofgifts/p/DVTclP_jZoJ/
- `tech-souvenir-set.webp` — Tech & Flask Souvenir Set. Inspiration: https://www.instagram.com/kokiesworldofgifts/reel/DbQj5OutPmJ/

These public posts were read in the browser. No Instagram photos were copied into the product catalogue. The new images are AI-generated concept illustrations. PNG originals are retained under `output/imagegen/` and WebP copies are consumed by the website. iDesire's fabric concepts remain exclusively in `lib/products.ts` and `public/products/concepts/`.
